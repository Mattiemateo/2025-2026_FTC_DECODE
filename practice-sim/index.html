    Robot calibration (driving + odometry)
    
Paste the Foresight Tuner or Two Wheel Tuner output, or all of pedro/Constants.java, and press Apply. Or type the numbers below. Saved in this browser.

    
    

    

    

    

      Braking: the TeleOp uses BRAKE, but the Foresight Tuner measures deceleration while coasting (FLOAT), so real stops are quicker.
      To measure braking: drive full speed (or 80% in the TeleOp), let go, measure the stopping distance d and use v² ÷ (2 × d).
      Coast deceleration is the lower limit; if both coast values are set, strafing brakes in the same ratio.

      Turn rate: spin in place at full right stick for 5 s and count turns (turns × 360 ÷ 5 × 1.25, because the TeleOp caps at 80%).

      Odometry: with Odometry drift set to "From ticks-to-inches", the simulated pods read stock goBILDA 4-Bar ticks (0.001979 in/tick) through your ticks-to-inches values, so a wrong value shows up as drift.
    

  


  

    About the simulation
    

      Copied from the biobuzz-season code: the drive math (drivePowers, 80% scale, robot-relative),
      intake and transfer power, the turret travel limits (−170° to +110°, clockwise positive), its 80°/s jog and hold-on-release,
      Limelight auto-aim with gyro hold, the search sweep, the shot lead for a moving robot, the ShotTable rows, and every
      auto-fire check in shotBlocker. Field coordinates and the start spot come from FieldAim.


      Driving speed, braking and turn rate come from Robot calibration above (guesses until the Pedro tuning is done). Guessed: flywheel spin-up time, the HIVE's footprint (approximate), ball size,
      capacity (3), and the CELL scoring radius. A shot lands at the distance the measured flywheel RPM gives in the shot table, so it scores
      when the RPM and aim are right, just like on the real robot once the table is tuned.
